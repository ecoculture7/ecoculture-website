Build a basket

Start with a basket. Change anything.

Pick the size closest to your household. It arrives filled in, and every line can be added to, reduced or removed. Everything here is priced per pack, so any quantity you can make is a quantity we can send.

One rule, enforced gently

At least one item from each of atta, dal, oil and spices. Rice is optional. It is what lets us plan a harvest instead of guessing, so the builder tells you when a category is empty — it never stops you editing.

Whole packs only

The first tap on anything adds a real pack, and each tap after adds another. There is no quantity on this page that we cannot pack and ship.

Prepaying is not a membership fee

The discount is the interest on money we receive early. Pay for more months at once and the discount grows. Membership is prepaid: there is no month-by-month option.
