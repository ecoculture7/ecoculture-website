What we sell, and what we claim

We sell staple foods: atta, rice, dal, cold-pressed oil and ground spices, sourced from farmers practising natural farming in Bundi, Rajasthan and Sant Kabir Nagar, Uttar Pradesh.

Our food is naturally grown. It is not certified organic, and we do not describe it as organic. The word "Organic" appears in our registered company name and nowhere else. If and when a certification is obtained we will say so plainly and show the certificate.

We name the farming collective and the district that grew each lot. We do not currently name individual farmers, because farmer-level traceability is still being built.

About the lab report

Every lot is tested for pesticide residue at a NABL-accredited laboratory before it ships, and the report travels with your order.

A lab report describes a sample drawn from that lot, tested for a stated list of substances, on a stated date. It is evidence about that lot. It is not a guarantee about every gram in your pack, it does not cover substances outside the tested list, and it is not a claim about nutrition or about health. We publish what the laboratory found, including when a lot fails.

If a lot fails its residue test, it does not ship. If we discover a failure after dispatch, we will tell you and refund you in full. See Refunds & cancellation.

Prices on this site are in Indian rupees. The maximum retail price printed on a pack is inclusive of all taxes, as Indian law requires. The price you pay is shown in full before you confirm an order, with delivery charges and any taxes itemised separately.

We may change prices for future cycles. If you have prepaid, the rate you prepaid at holds for your whole term, whatever happens in the market after. We will tell you before a price change affects you.

Where a pack shows a struck-through shelf price beside our price, that struck-through figure is what comparable products sell for elsewhere, not a price we have ever charged. How the MRP is set.

Orders and cycles

We deliver on a monthly cycle. Each cycle has a cut-off date of [ cut-off ]. Until the cut-off you can change, skip or cancel that cycle. After the cut-off the lot is committed to you and the cycle will ship.

Every order must contain at least one item from each of atta, dal, oil and spices (rice is optional), and every quantity must be a whole pack. The builder on this site will not let you create an order that cannot be packed.

If a harvest falls short

This food comes from specific fields in specific seasons. If a crop fails or falls short, members are served before anyone else, in the order their commitment was received. If we cannot supply an item you have paid for, we will offer you a substitute, a credit against your next cycle, or a refund for that item. Your choice.

We will not quietly substitute a different variety or a different origin for what you ordered.
