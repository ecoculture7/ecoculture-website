Refunds & cancellation

Draft. This page is not final until it has been reviewed by the company's CA and legal adviser. Anything in [ square brackets ] is still to be decided.

If something arrives wrong

If an item is missing, damaged, leaking, stale, or not what the pack says it is, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and send a photograph. We will replace it or refund it, whichever you prefer. You do not have to return the item unless we ask, and if we do ask, we pay for the collection.

Write to [ grievance officer email ] or message +91 81780 30060 on WhatsApp with your name, your area, and what went wrong. We will give you a ticket number.

If a lot fails its residue test

A lot that fails does not ship. If we discover a failure after your order has reached you, we will contact you, ask you to stop using it, collect it at our cost and refund you in full, whether or not you have opened it, and whether or not you complained.

Food you have opened

Because this is food, we cannot take back packs that have been opened or used, except for a quality or safety problem as described above. This does not affect your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

Skipping or cancelling a cycle

You can skip a cycle or cancel it entirely at any time before the cut-off date, which is [ cut-off ] each month. There is no charge and no notice period. If you have already paid for that cycle, we refund it in full.

After the cut-off, the lot has been committed to you and the cycle will ship. If you tell us after the cut-off, we will do what we can, but we cannot promise it.

Cancelling a prepaid membership

You can stop at any time, and what comes back depends on whether the harvest your money funded has already been bought.

Before we buy that harvest, your prepayment is fully refundable. The months you have already taken are recalculated at the rate for the term you actually used, and the rest is returned. So if you prepaid twelve months at the twelve-month discount off and stop after three, those three months are recharged at the three-month rate of the three-month discount off and everything else comes back.

After we have bought it, the money is no longer ours to return. It is standing crop, paid to farmers at the weigh-in. That is the whole point of prepaying, and it is why the discount exists. From that point you can still stop taking deliveries, transfer your membership to someone else, or pause and resume within the season, but we cannot refund the harvest already purchased in your name.

We will always tell you the date of the next harvest purchase before you prepay, and again before it happens.

We never charge a cancellation fee.

The trial pack

A trial pack is paid for when it ships and is covered by the same 48-hour rule above. Its price is credited in full against any prepaid term taken within 30 days; the credit is not paid out in cash.

If something changes mid-season

Your prepayment buys a number of monthly cycles, not a fixed box. Most changes cost nothing and need no permission.

Change your basket, any month. Switch between Small, Medium and Large, or change any item, before the cut-off. If your new basket costs more, pay the difference at your member rate. If it costs less, the balance stays on your account. Your term and your discount do not change, because the discount was priced on how long you committed, not on how much you eat.

Pause. Up to [ 2 ] cycles in a term, for travel, illness, or any reason at all. Your term extends by the same number of months. Tell us before the cut-off.

Move within Delhi NCR. Send us the new address before the cut-off. Nothing else changes.

Move out of Delhi NCR. We cannot deliver outside NCR yet. You have three choices: transfer your membership to someone here, have the remaining cycles delivered to someone you nominate in NCR, or stop and take a refund under the terms above. Transfers are free and we handle them.

Stop because we got it wrong. If a lot failed its residue test, or the food was not what we said it was, you get your whole unused balance back: no recalculation, no deduction, whether or not we have already bought the harvest. That is on us, not on you.

Transfer to someone else. Free, any time, to anyone we can deliver to.

These rules are the same on Membership.

How and when we refund

Refunds go back to the method you paid with, within [ number ] working days of our approving the refund. [ Payment gateway ] may take a further [ number ] days to show it in your account. If you would rather have a credit against your next cycle, say so and we will do that instead. It is usually faster.

If we cannot supply what you paid for

If a crop falls short, members are served first. If we still cannot supply an item you have paid for, you choose: a substitute, a credit, or a refund for that item.

If you are not satisfied with our answer

Our grievance officer is named on the Contact page. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within one month. You may also approach a consumer forum where you live, or the National Consumer Helpline on 1915.
