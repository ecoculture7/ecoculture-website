Membership

You're not subscribing to a box. You're funding a harvest.

A box subscription buys food that already exists. Membership is different. You pay ahead for three, six or twelve months, and that money goes to a farmer as a promise, before the season: sow this, and we will buy it. It is paid out at the weigh-in, the lot is sealed in your name, and it reaches your kitchen month by month with its lab report.

There is no membership fee. The discount is what we pay you for the use of your money, and the longer you prepay, the more it is worth. There is no month-by-month option, because a month at a time cannot buy a harvest.

Where your money goes

  1. First

    You prepay

    Three, six or twelve months of your basket, at a discount.

  2. Before sowing

    A farmer is told what to sow, and promised a buyer

    A price fixed before the seed goes in. No loan from the input dealer, because natural farming makes its inputs on the farm.

  3. At harvest

    Paid at the weigh-in, with your money

    On the day the crop is weighed, at the price promised. Not the mandi's price, and not months later.

  4. Then

    Sealed in your name, tested, delivered

    The lot is recorded against the members whose money bought it, a sample goes to a NABL laboratory, and it reaches you month by month with the report.

Everyone has to win, or it does not work

For you

Staples tested every harvest, priced below the certified-organic shelf, cheaper still for paying ahead, and locked for the season.

For the farmer

A buyer before sowing and cash at the weigh-in. No loan for inputs, no mandi price. A farmer paid like this can afford to stay natural.

For the soil

A season farmed without bought chemicals. Each one puts life back: more organic matter, more worms, more water held.

What membership gives you.

Cheaper, every month

The discount is interest on money we get early: a little for three months, more for six, the most for twelve.

Your price is locked

The rate you prepay at holds for the whole season, whatever the mandi does after.

You come first

A natural harvest can fall short. When it does, members are served before anyone else.

From a set amount of produce, which every starting basket clears. Read before your discount, so prepaying never costs you the free drop.

If we get it wrong, it all comes back

A failed test or food that is not what we said: your whole unused balance is returned, no questions.

If you need to stop.

You can. What comes back depends on whether the harvest your money funded has been bought yet, and on why you are stopping. The rules are below, and they are the same on Refunds & cancellation.

The rules

If something changes mid-season.

Your prepayment buys a number of monthly cycles, not a fixed box. Most changes cost nothing and need no permission.

Change your basket, any month. Switch between Small, Medium and Large, or change any item, before the cut-off. If your new basket costs more, pay the difference at your member rate. If it costs less, the balance stays on your account. Your term and your discount do not change, because the discount was priced on how long you committed, not on how much you eat.

Pause. Up to 2 cycles in a term, for travel, illness, or any reason at all. Your term extends by the same number of months. Tell us before the cut-off.

Move within Delhi NCR. Send us the new address before the cut-off. Nothing else changes.

Move out of Delhi NCR. We cannot deliver outside NCR yet. You have three choices: transfer your membership to someone here, have the remaining cycles delivered to someone you nominate in NCR, or stop and take a refund under the terms below. Transfers are free and we handle them.

Stop because we got it wrong. If a lot failed its residue test, or the food was not what we said it was, you get your whole unused balance back: no recalculation, no deduction, whether or not we have already bought the harvest. That is on us, not on you.

Stop for any other reason. Before we buy the harvest your money funded, your unused balance is returned, with the months you have taken recalculated at the rate for the term you actually used. After we have bought it, that money is standing crop already paid to farmers at the weigh-in: you can still transfer, nominate an address, or pause, but we cannot return it. We tell you the harvest purchase date before you prepay, and again before it happens.

Transfer to someone else. Free, any time, to anyone we can deliver to.
