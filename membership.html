Membership

You're not subscribing to a box. You're joining a harvest.

There is no membership fee. You pay for your food in advance, that money buys the harvest at the weigh-in, and the discount is what we pay you for the use of it. The longer you prepay, the more it is worth, which is why the discount is not flat.

Prefer to pay month by month? That is a perfectly good way to buy. You pay the list price and you are free to stop.

The discount ladder.

It is the cost of money, not a loyalty tier. Pay for more months at once and the discount grows.

How delivery counts toward it.

Delivery is free above a set amount of produce. We read that on the list price, before any prepayment discount. Otherwise the discount would push you further from free delivery, which would be a strange sort of benefit.
