The lab report

A certificate says a system was followed. A lab report says what's in your bag.

The organic staples most people already doubt are certified. The logo is on the pack, and it still hasn't settled the question. So we'd rather hand you the actual test.

Because we buy from two districts, the report can name the collective that grew its lot, and the district. A lab report from an anonymous supply chain is a claim. One attached to a named collective is evidence.

What you receive

One report per lot. It travels with your order.

Each report carries a lot code, the result, where and when it was tested, what was screened for, and then the trail back: which collective grew it, in which district, when it was harvested, when it was milled and what was paid at the weigh-in.

The report is the claim. Everything below it is how you check that it is about your food and not somebody else's.

Start to finish

How a lot is verified.

1 · At the farm

The weigh-in

The crop is weighed and recorded against the farmer's name and field, and the farmer is paid at that weigh-in. The lot gets its code there.

2 · Sampled

Right after harvest, to the lab

A sample from the lot goes to a NABL-accredited laboratory for a residue panel. A retained sample stays with us.

3 · Sealed and stored

Sealed and stored

The lot is sealed and stored, and recorded against the members whose commitment bought it.

4 · Processed

Processed before it ships

Atta is milled within a week of shipping. Dal and spices are processed in batches that last two to three months. The report for that lot goes out with your order.

Naturally grown. Not organic.

We hold no certification and claim none. Until PGS certification is in hand we say naturally grown. The word "organic" appears in our registered company name and nowhere else.

What a clean report does not prove

It shows that the screened residues were not detected in that sample, on that day. It does not describe the farm's every practice. That is why we also name the collective that grew it, so you can ask.

If a lot fails

A lot that fails does not ship. If we find out after it has reached you, we collect it at our cost and refund you in full, opened or not. See Refunds & cancellation.
