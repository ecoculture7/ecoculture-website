## 4. CORRECTED — the mission-led homepage was the founder's decision

The first version of this report argued the homepage had reversed the survey data without a
founder deciding. That was wrong on both counts.

**It was the founder's decision, and the reasoning holds:** the survey measured what makes
people *trust* a claim, not what makes them *buy*. Nobody buys staples because of a lab report.
The lab report builds confidence and retention; the story is what sells. Treating a trust
metric as an acquisition metric was an error in the first report.

**Do not revert the mission-led structure.** The refinement already agreed is section 0.1 —
making the opening specific to Ecoculture rather than a claim any natural-farming brand could
make.

## 6. Retracted — do not act on these

1. **"The homepage is blank below the hero."** An artifact of full-page screenshot capture —
   the reveal is scroll-driven and no scroll events fire during a stitched capture.
2. **"Small basket total ₹1,731 is wrong by ₹2."** The checker was wrong. Python's `round()` is
   banker's rounding (130.5 → 130); JavaScript's `Math.round` is half-up (130.5 → 131).
3. **"build.html has four 18×18 tap targets."** Measured the `<input>` instead of its wrapping
   `<label>`. The real target is 304×44.
4. **"The 24 October prices and the mission-led homepage were unapproved."** Both were the
   founder's decisions. See 1.3 and 4.
