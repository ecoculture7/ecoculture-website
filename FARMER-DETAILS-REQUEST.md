# Farmer details we need from PSF

For `/sourcing`, `/farmers` and the lot record on `/proof`. The old site's farmer
profiles were placeholders, so **nothing on the new site is taken from them**.
Every `[ bracket ]` on the new pages is waiting for one of the answers below.

**Three to five farmers is enough to launch.** Pick people who are growing the
crops in our baskets (wheat, rice, mustard, dals, spices) and who are happy to
be named. Please do not send anyone who has not agreed to be named.

## Per farmer

Ask each farmer's permission first, and record that they said yes.

| # | What we need | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full name, as they want it written | The site names every farmer |
| 2 | Village, block and district | Every field gets a village |
| 3 | Land under natural farming, in acres or bigha (say which) | Shown beside the name |
| 4 | Which of our crops they grow, and how much they expect to supply this season | Ties a farmer to an item |
| 5 | Years farming; years farming without chemical inputs | Honest history, not a slogan |
| 6 | What they use for fertility and pest control (their own words) | We say "naturally grown" and must be able to back it |
| 7 | What changed for them, in one or two sentences, in their own words | The only thing a visitor remembers |
| 8 | Whether they are a member of the producer company or a self-help group, and which one | Trace the chain |
| 9 | Photo of the farmer and of the field, taken for this purpose, with consent to publish | Replaces every `PLACEHOLDER-*` image |
| 10 | A phone number we can call (not published) | Our team can verify the profile |

Photos should show the person and the place, not a posed stock-style shot.
Landscape, at least 1600 px wide, taken in daylight.

## About the producer company and the two districts

1. Registered name of the producer company in Bundi, and its registration number.
2. Is it women-led? How many farming families are members, and how many will
   supply Ecoculture this season? (The site currently says "women-led" and
   leaves the number as `[ number ]`.)
3. What do the self-help groups do — cleaning, grading, anything else — and
   where?
4. Where is the oil pressed (ghani name and place), and where are atta and
   spices milled and ground?
5. Who supplies Kalanamak in Sant Kabir Nagar? The site says fifteen farmers
   we trained ourselves; please confirm the number and send the same details as
   above for at least two of them.
6. **Arhar:** is there a named farmer or group in the Banda track yet? If not,
   it comes off the basket.
7. What is the weigh-in process: where, who records it, and how is the farmer
   paid (cash, transfer) and how quickly?
8. Distance from Bundi to Delhi NCR by road. The site currently says 600 km;
   please confirm the real figure.
9. Harvest months for each crop, so the "harvest" labels are right.

## Please send back

- A short sheet (or WhatsApp voice notes, transcribed) with the answers above.
- The photos, named `firstname-village.jpg` — never overwrite an old file name.
- Written consent from each named farmer to publish their name, village, photo
  and quotation.
