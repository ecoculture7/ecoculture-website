### 1.3 Nineteen MRP rows were flipped to `confirmed: true`, and every MRP now equals the selling price

`data/prices.js` lines 46–64. Every SKU price also changed on 24 October under the commit
message *"Prices aligned to the pouch artwork for nine items."*

| SKU | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| atta | 78 | 82 |
| bansi | 138 | **105** |
| sonamoti | 188 | 158 |
| basmati | 158 | 166 |
| kalanamak | **299** | **314** |
| arhar | 248 | 261 |
| moong | 230 | 242 |
| chana | 185 | 195 |
| masoor | 245 | **210** |

Three problems, in order of seriousness:

1. **Kalanamak was the one price the founder confirmed personally** — MRP ₹399, selling ₹299.
   It now reads `sells: 314, mrp: 314, confirmed: true`. A confirmed figure was overwritten
   and the flag that vouches for it was left on.
2. **`confirmed: true` is now set on 19 rows.** That flag exists so an unsigned-off MRP cannot
   reach a pack. An agent set it. Unless the CA has signed this set off, set them back to
   `false` and leave `provisional: true`.
3. **The artwork is driving the data.** `data/prices.js` is the source of truth; pouch artwork
   is downstream of it. If the artwork is right, change the data deliberately and record why
   — don't reconcile the data to the artwork.

Setting MRP equal to the selling price may well be the right call: it removes any
inflated-MRP exposure and suits a brand whose argument is that it doesn't play pricing games.
But it is a founder decision, not a refactor.

## 6. Retracted from an earlier draft of this report

Recorded so nobody acts on them:

1. **"The homepage is blank below the hero."** An artifact of full-page screenshot capture —
   the reveal is scroll-driven and no scroll events fire during a stitched capture. Real
   browsers are fine.
2. **"Small basket total ₹1,731 is wrong by ₹2."** The checker was wrong. Python's `round()`
   is banker's rounding (130.5 → 130); JavaScript's `Math.round` is half-up (130.5 → 131).
   The page is right.
3. **"build.html has four 18×18 tap targets."** Measured the `<input>` instead of its wrapping
   `<label>`. The real target is 304×44.

## 7. Constraints that override anything above

- **Never** call Ecoculture's own food "organic". Only the registered entity
  (*Bodh Organic Ecoculture Pvt Ltd*) and the category take that word. Our food is
  **naturally grown**.
- **No stock photography, ever.** Every face has a name, every field a village. A placeholder
  must be visibly a placeholder.
- No nutrition figures without a lab report. **No carbon or environmental comparison at all.**
  No health claims.

  without a matching `data-sku`/`data-price` pair is a bug.
- An MRP may not reach a page or a pack while `provisional: true` or `confirmed: false`.
