If your pincode is not on this list, tell us where you are and we will let you know when we reach you. We would rather say no than deliver badly.

When we deliver

One delivery a month, on a fixed cycle. Your order is dispatched after the cycle cut-off of [ cut-off ], and reaches you between [ window ]. We will message you the day before.

We are not a ten-minute service, and that is deliberate. Ordering on a cycle is what lets us buy a whole harvest at the weigh-in and pay farmers at that scale.

Who brings it

In some areas we deliver ourselves. In others a delivery partner brings it. Either way the pack is sealed and carries its lot code, and the lab report for that lot reaches you by email.

If nobody is home

We will call before we arrive. If we cannot reach you, we will try again [ number of attempts and when ]. We will not leave food with a neighbour or outside your door unless you have told us to, in writing or on WhatsApp.

If delivery fails because the address or the phone number was wrong, or because nobody was available after [ number ] attempts, we may charge the small-order delivery cost on a redelivery.

Checking your order

Please check the packs when they arrive. If anything is missing, damaged or wrong, tell us within 48 hours. See Refunds & cancellation.

Things outside our control

Weather, a failed harvest, a transport strike or a public restriction can delay a cycle. If a delivery will be late we will tell you as soon as we know, and you can take it late or take a refund for that cycle.
