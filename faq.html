Questions

What people ask before they order.

If yours is not here, write to admin@ecoculture.in or message us on WhatsApp. A person answers.

Is it organic?

No, and we do not say it is. We hold no certification and claim none. Until PGS certification is in hand we say naturally grown. The word "organic" appears in our registered company name and nowhere else.

What backs the claim is a residue report for every lot, naming the farmer and the field. See how.

What does the lab test actually show?

Each lot is sampled and sent to a NABL-accredited laboratory for a residue panel. The report shows whether the screened residues were detected in that sample. It does not describe every practice on the farm, which is why we also name the farmer.

What if a lot fails the test?

We ask for the pack to be returned and refund you in full. The details are on the refunds page.

Where does it come from?

Two districts: Bundi in Rajasthan, and Sant Kabir Nagar in Uttar Pradesh for Kalanamak rice. Item by item, here.

Arhar dal is the one open source. We are working a Banda-district track and will name the farmer before it ships, or it will not be in the basket.

Do I have to pay now?

No. Reserving a trial pack takes no payment. We write once when prices are set, and once more when it ships.

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

Delhi NCR only, until we can do it well. Delivery is free above a set amount of produce. Between a lower amount and that it is a small fee, and below the lower amount it is a larger fee, because packing and the drop cost us about the same on any order.

The full explanation is on Shipping & delivery.

Do I have to take everything?

No. You pick what you want. The one rule is at least one item from each of atta, dal, oil and spices. Rice is optional. Build a basket and the builder will tell you if a category is empty.

Why are the pack sizes fixed?

The first of anything you add is a real pack, not a spoonful. Atta and Basmati come in 1 kg, Kalanamak and the dals in 500 g, oil in 500 ml and spices in 100 g. Every quantity the builder produces is a whole number of packs.

Is there a membership fee?

No. If you prepay, you receive a discount, which is what we pay for the use of your money. If you prefer to pay month by month, you pay the list price. How it works.

I am a farmer. Can I supply you?

Possibly. We are working in two districts for now, but we want to hear from you. किसान साझेदारी (हिन्दी)
