Pricing Set for December

Farm-gate rates closed with the producer company in October, so these are the December prices, not estimates. They hold for the season your membership covers, and prepaying a year takes 12% off. Kalanamak at ₹299 a kilo is our own crop — a 500 g pack is in every basket at ₹150, and the ₹399 MRP on the pack is what it would cost through a shop.

Pack sizes

The first of anything you add is a real pack, not a spoonful. Atta and Basmati come in 1 kg, Kalanamak in 500 g, dals in 500 g, oil in 500 ml, spices in 100 g.

We dropped the 50 g spice sachet. At ₹34 it costs us more to print the pouch and fill it than it earns, and pretending otherwise would just move the loss somewhere you cannot see it.

Arhar is the one open source

Our Bundi partner does not grow it. We are working a Banda-district track and will name the farmer before it ships — or it will not be in the basket. It is listed here because we expect to have it, not because we do.

Three wheats, three prices

Standard at ₹78, Bansi at ₹138, Sona Moti at ₹188. They are different wheats that cost different amounts to grow, and the pack says which one you have. Most brands sell one thing called "atta".

Under ₹1,200 we charge ₹249, and we will show you why. Packing, the lab retain and one van drop cost us about ₹320 whether the order is ₹600 or ₹6,000. A ₹600 order does not cover that. We could bury it in the product price so every customer pays for it, or name it.

There is one threshold and it is the same for everyone. Prepaying takes 2% off at three months, 5% at six and 12% at twelve, and we read the ₹2,500 against the list price rather than the discounted one — otherwise the discount would push you further from free delivery, which would be a strange sort of benefit.

Half our respondents are one- or two-person households, whose natural monthly order lands under the threshold. The fix is not a smaller basket — it is a longer cycle. Atta keeps two months, dal and oil keep six. Order two months at once and a small household crosses ₹2,500 on its own, with no surcharge, no discount and one van trip instead of two.
