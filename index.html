How you'll know

A certificate says a system was followed. A lab report says what's in your bag.

The organic staples most people already doubt are certified — the logo is right there on the pack, and it still hasn't settled the question. So we'd rather hand you the actual test.

And because we buy from two districts rather than twenty states, that report can name the field it came from. A lab report from an anonymous supply chain is a claim. One attached to a named plot is evidence.

Membership

You're not subscribing to a box. You're joining a harvest.

There is no membership fee. You pay for your food in advance, that money buys the harvest at the weigh-in, and the discount is what we pay you for the use of it. The longer you prepay, the more it is worth — which is why the discount is not flat.

A box subscription

The food is bought after you order.

  1. You order
  2. Stock is bought to fill the orders
  3. Packed from that stock
  4. Delivered

Joining a harvest

The food is bought before the season, by your commitment.

  1. You commit, and prepay if you choose
  2. Farmers are told what to sow, and promised we will buy it
  3. At the weigh-in, the commitment buys the harvest
  4. Sealed as a lot, recorded against your name
  5. Delivered, month by month
