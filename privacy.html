The short version

We collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and what you ordered, so we can bring you food and talk to you about it.

We also use measurement and advertising tools that set cookies and tell us how people use this site. Those only run if you say yes. You can say no, or change your mind later, and the site works exactly the same either way. Nothing we do is required for you to buy from us.

We have never sold anyone's data and we will not.

Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file a website leaves in your browser. We group ours in three, and you control two of the three.

1. Strictly necessary, always on, no consent needed. Without these the site cannot do what you asked. They remember your basket, keep a form submission secure, and record which cookies you agreed to.

2. Measurement, off until you say yes. These tell us which pages people read and where they give up. We use them to fix the site, not to build a profile of you.

3. Advertising, off until you say yes. These let us see whether an advertisement led to an order, and may let the advertising platform recognise your browser elsewhere.

What we still do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We do not use your data to train any automated or AI system.
  • We do not make automated decisions about you.
  • We do not track you across other people's websites for our own purposes.
  • We do not advertise to children, or knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18.

Who else sees your data

  • Delivery. Where we deliver ourselves, nobody outside Ecoculture sees your address. Where a partner delivers, they receive your name, address and phone number, only what is needed to reach your door.
  • Payment. Handled by [ payment gateway ]. We never receive or store your full card or bank details.
  • Hosting and email. Amazon Web Services, in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region, ap-south-1: our records and our order emails. Your data stays in India.
  • Measurement and advertising, only if you consented. [ analytics provider ] and [ advertising platform ] receive your IP address, your device and browser, and which of our pages you saw. These companies process data outside India and use it for their own purposes as well as ours, under their own privacy policies. This is the main reason the choice is yours to make.
  • Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • The laboratory that tests our food receives samples and lot codes, and nothing about you.
  • We disclose data where a law or a court requires it, and we tell you unless we are forbidden.

Data that leaves India

Our hosting and email are in India. Measurement and advertising providers process data outside India. Where that happens we rely on the conditions the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules set for transfers, and on the provider's own contractual terms. If you refuse measurement and advertising cookies, your data does not leave India.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to:

  • show you what we hold about you and who we have shared it with;
  • correct anything inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • delete what we hold, where we are not required by law to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time: for cookies, through Manage cookies in the footer; for anything else, by writing to us. It is as easy to withdraw as it was to give;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapable.

We respond within [ 30 ] days. There is no charge.

Children

This service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, we do not profile children, and we do not direct advertising at them, which the DPDP Act prohibits. If you believe a child has given us data, tell us and we will delete it.

Keeping it safe

Access is limited to the people who need it to do their job. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. If a breach occurs that affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India within the time the DPDP Rules require, including what happened and what we are doing.

Farmers

We publish a farmer's name, village, photograph or words only with their written consent, which they can withdraw.

Complaints

Write to our grievance officer, named on the Contact page. We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within one month. If we do not resolve it to your satisfaction, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
